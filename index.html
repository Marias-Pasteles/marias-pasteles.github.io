Our story

Made the way abuela taught us

Every family gathering in Puerto Rico starts in the kitchen: grating guineo, seasoning the meat, and wrapping pasteles in banana leaves with the whole family.

Maria's brings that tradition to your table, one dozen at a time, so you can spend less time cooking and more time celebrating.
